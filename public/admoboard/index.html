AdMoboard

Your Google AdMob earnings, beautifully — a native iOS dashboard.

iOS 18+ Native SwiftUI On-device intelligence

What it does

  • Earnings at a glance — today, yesterday, last 7 days, this month and last month, with honest comparisons against the same days of the previous period.
  • Breakdowns — see which apps, ad units and countries earn the most, and drill into any of them.
  • Home-screen widget — today's figure without opening the app.
  • Ask — ask questions like "How much did I earn yesterday?" in plain language. Questions are interpreted on your device with Apple Intelligence; every figure in every answer is computed by the app from your own report. Nothing leaves your iPhone.
  • Multiple accounts, Face ID lock, offline cache — your data stays available and protected.

How it works with your Google account

AdMoboard signs in with your Google account and reads your AdMob reports through the official AdMob API using the read-only scope (admob.readonly). It can never modify your AdMob configuration. Reports are fetched directly from Google to your device, displayed to you, and cached only on your device. AdMoboard has no servers and shares your data with no one.
